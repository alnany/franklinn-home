🛡️ Account Shield

Your accounts don't get banned on our watch.

Most mass translation tools trigger bot detection — messages too fast, too similar, too robotic. Franklinn is built from the ground up to look human to every platform.

⚡

Human-paced delivery

Adaptive delays and typing indicators match each platform's organic patterns — no burst sends, no suspicious uniformity.

🌐

Residential IP routing

Each Seat is pinned to a clean residential IP in your region. No datacenter fingerprints, no geo-anomalies.
